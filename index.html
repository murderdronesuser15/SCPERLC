Command Usage

⚠️
Important for New Staff

Please read this page and others carefully — you will be quizzed on them!

Command Letter Requirements

Commands in the game are prefixed with : (colon). The number of letters after the colon determines the rank required to use the command.

3+ Letters4+ LettersHalf UsernameFull Username
:to:bring:kick:ban
:view:kill:mod:unmod
:down:admin:unadmin
:tp:helper
:refresh:unhelper
:heal
:wanted
:unwanted
:jail
:respawn
:load
⛔
CRITICAL — DO NOT USE

DO NOT USE COMMANDS THAT TARGET "ALL" OR "OTHERS". Staff must be aware that player usernames can actually include the words "all" or "others" (e.g., a player could be named "all", "others", "allies", "otherside", etc.). Using a command that targets all or others could accidentally target real players whose names contain those words, resulting in unintended bans, kicks, or other punishments. Always target specific usernames instead.

Command Categories

Movement Commands

  • :to — Teleport to a player
  • :tp — Teleport to a location
  • :bring — Bring a player to you
  • :load — Load a player's character

Moderation Commands

  • :kick — Kick a player from the server
  • :ban — Ban a player permanently
  • :mod — Promote a player to moderator
  • :unmod — Remove moderator status
  • :admin — Promote to admin
  • :unadmin — Remove admin status
  • :helper — Promote to helper
  • :unhelper — Remove helper status

Gameplay Commands

  • :kill — Kill a player
  • :heal — Heal a player
  • :refresh — Refresh a player's character
  • :respawn — Respawn a player
  • :down — Down a player
  • :wanted — Mark player as wanted
  • :unwanted — Clear wanted status
  • :jail — Jail a player
  • :view — Spectate a player

Banned Roleplay

⚠️
Banned Roleplay

We ask you to moderate any Roleplay against the Roblox Platform's Terms of Service as specified in the Punishments section of this guide.

Examples of Banned Roleplay

  • Bomb Roleplay — Simulating bomb threats or explosive-related scenarios
  • Torture Roleplay — Any form of torture or severe abuse RP
  • NSFW Roleplay — Not Safe For Work content
  • Extremism — Recreating tragic real-world events (e.g., 9/11)
  • Racist Roleplay — Racist behavior or language

General On-Duty Guidelines

  • Always maintain professionalism while on duty
  • Follow the chain of command at all times
  • Document all moderation actions taken
  • Be fair and consistent with punishments
  • Do not abuse your commands or powers

Staff Policies

ℹ️
Core Rules

These are the core rules of Staff. Please read them slowly and carefully, as they include some of the most important rules. Breach of conduct won't be taken lightly and may result in harsh punishments.

Staff Code of Conduct

1. Professionalism

All staff members are expected to conduct themselves professionally at all times, both in-game and out-of-game. This includes using appropriate language, respecting all community members, and not engaging in personal conflicts while on duty.

2. Fairness

Punishments must be applied consistently and fairly. Do not show favoritism to any player, including friends or people you know outside the game.

3. Transparency

Always provide a reason when issuing a punishment. Players have the right to know why they were punished.

4. No Command Abuse

Using moderation commands for personal gain, harassment, or entertainment is strictly prohibited and will result in severe consequences.

5. Chain of Command

Always respect the hierarchy. Report issues to your superiors and follow their instructions.

6. Confidentiality

Do not share sensitive staff information, internal discussions, or punishment records with players or the general public.

President Roleplay

ℹ️
What is President Roleplay?

President Roleplay is the main roleplay type on our server, simulating the role of the President of the United States. It involves managing the President's schedule, protecting them, and ensuring a realistic and engaging experience for all participants.

About This Guide

This guide is divided into smaller pages for better readability. You can always come back to check something, even while hosting the roleplay!

💡
Tip

The most important thing is that you know how to do what you're asked to do in-game. Don't worry if everything doesn't click right away — you can always reference this guide during hosting.

Quick Navigation

  • Overview — General information about President RP

  • Procedures — Step-by-step procedures for hosting
  • Player Roles — Player role subdivision
  • Convoy Formation — Convoy rules and procedures

Key Elements

  • Presidential Schedule — Managing the President's daily activities and movements
  • Security — Protecting the President from threats
  • Convoy — Organized vehicle movement with the President
  • Public Engagement — Interacting with citizens and managing public relations

President Roleplay — Roles and Tasks

Role Subdivision

To ensure an organized hosting of the President RP, our server adopts a role subdivision for our on-duty staff members. To host a President RP, at least 2 (advised 3) staff members must be on-duty, and 10 (advised 15) players must be online.

Role Assignment

Roles can be assigned in two ways:

  1. Designated by High Ranking staff — Decided by an HR+ staff member, or a Middle Ranking staff member delegated by HR+
  2. Automatic assignment — The highest on-duty rank below HR ranks becomes the host; Trial Moderators cannot be hosts

Staff Roles

The Host and Co-Host

  • Supervise and manage all aspects of the President RP
  • Manage staff-player communications about the RP
  • Make frequent announcements about the RP situation
  • Check that everyone is following the rules
  • Coordinate RP procedures and make decisions
  • Lead the convoy and choose its route
  • Confirm or invalidate kills of the President
  • Handle President replacement if they go offline

The Supporters

  • Complete side-tasks assigned by the host/co-host
  • Answer players' questions about the RP
  • Monitor the President for any kills
  • Help decide whether kills are valid
  • Assist with convoy formation if asked

The Moderation Subdivision

  • Respond to mod calls
  • Answer players' questions on any matter
  • Patrol to check cars are parked correctly
  • Ensure players are following server rules
  • Follow orders from HR+ staff members
  • Supervise Whitelisted RPs if asked
  • Ensure players are wearing realistic avatars

President Roleplay — Procedures

Remember!

You can click on the appropriate button on commands boxes to instantly copy them and paste them in-game!

Locations

The President can go everywhere he decides, but cannot go to:

  • Any safe zone
  • Police Department, Fire Department, Sheriff's Office
  • Gun Store, Prison, Any Garage
  • High Rock Park watchtower, Farm silo, Civilian spawn points

The President must stay in a location for at least 5 minutes and should change every 15 minutes. The first location must always be the White House (Hospital).

Changing Location

When the President asks to change location, ensure he has passed at least 5 minutes in the current location and that the location is not banned. Set up a peace timer of 500 seconds:

Peacetimer
:pt 500

Then announce:

Location Announcement
:h The President is now going to [location]. Please form a convoy outside [your current location].

When the President Dies

Each President has 3 lives. When he dies, he loses 1 life. Announce the death:

Death Announcement
:h The President [username] has died. He has now [remaining lives]/3 lives remaining!

Then revive the President:

Revive
:heal [username]

Set up a 60-second peace timer:

Organize Peace Timer
:pt 60

Emergency Escape

The President can emergency-escape with Host approval:

Escape Announcement
:h The President is making an emergency escape!
Arrival Announcement
:h The President has made an emergency escape and is now at [Location].

President Roleplay — Player Roles

Player Role Subdivision

Players in President RP divide into the following roles:

  • The President — The main character, has 3 lives, decides where to go and what to do
  • The Vice-President — Second-most elected, serves as replacement if President goes offline
  • The Guards — Players who spontaneously decide to protect the President
  • The LEOs — Police Officers, Sheriffs, EMS, Fire Department, DOT
  • The Civilians — Everyone else, may spectate or try to kill the President

The President

The President is the main character of this Roleplay type. This role is the representation of the real-life "President of the United States of America". The President is elected through elections by the players. The President has 3 lives, meaning he can be killed only 3 times in 1 RP. The RP revolves around the President.

The Vice-President

The Vice-President is the second-most elected candidate at the elections. He later serves as the replacement for an unavailable President. In this RP, the Vice-President has no power on anything, nor can decide what to do and where to go. He can only decide to remain and follow the President in his moves, or play as a normal player until it's his turn to become President.

The Guards

The Guards are a small group of players who spontaneously decide to become the personal Guards of the President. These are not regulated by the staff of the server, as their job is spontaneous and they can decide what to do at any time. A President can also have no Guards at all.

The LEOs

LEO stands for Low Enforcement Officer. This is the group that represents Police Officers and Sheriffs Officers (people who enforce the law), and sometimes also include EMS and Fire Department people. Police Officers and Sheriffs Officers are able to participate in the RP as they want and if they want. EMS and Fire Department serve their normal role of extinguishing fires and healing people. DOT members can participate and are welcome to set up the convoy's path barriers.

The Civilians

The civilians are the remaining people who choose not to play as LEO or don't become President/Vice-President. Civilians may choose to do one of two things: attack the President, or roleplay peacefully. Everyone can switch between roles as many times as they want and whenever they want during the RP, apart from the elected roles which are the President and Vice-President.

President Roleplay — Convoy Formation

Convoy Rules and Constraints

A convoy must:

  • Not have more than 8 non-staff vehicles (usually)
  • Have a maximum of 2 limousines, one which carries both the President and Vice-President, the other being the decoy
  • Have a maximum of 2 staff vehicles, one at the very front, and the other at the very back
  • Not have any civilians in the convoy
  • Be composed of only blacked out vehicles, meaning that all vehicles must have a black livery without any writing on them (an exception is made for Whitelisted Departments vehicles)

During Travel

  • No overtaking unless the vehicle in front is stuck, AFK, or if a staff member gives permission
  • No sirens, honking, or ramming into other vehicles
  • All convoy roads must be fully blocked to prevent any external disturbance
  • No civilian should drive in or around the convoy
  • Maintain a speed between 15-30 mph, or 15-30 Km/h

Convoy Procedures

During a convoy you should always make sure that a Peace Timer is active to prevent anyone from shooting at the convoy:

Active Peace Timer
:pt 500

The President cannot be killed during a convoy. If the President's vehicle decides to drive off and away without staff consent, the President can then be killed, but you must stop the convoy immediately and teleport to his vehicle to get him back on track.

SCP Roleplay — Procedures

Remember!

You can click on the appropriate button on commands boxes to instantly copy them and paste them in-game!

SCP Setup

Recommended Staff/Volunteer Count: SCP Roleplay works best with 3–5+ staff members or volunteers to ensure proper coverage and engagement.

Avatar Requirements: All participating staff and volunteers must have avatar setups that relate to SCP themes (e.g., Foundation uniforms, MTF gear, civilian clothing appropriate to the scenario).

Before starting the roleplay, you need to find either a staff member or volunteer who will go on the civilian team and acquire a weapon of some sort. This person will serve as the target for the scenario.

Once the convoy is prepared, you may start the journey to one of the approved locations (Hospital, Barns, County Jail, Mansion at 601, or Airport).

For the Hospital location, pull all the way in front of the main entrance past the overpass hang.

The appropriate teams should start securing the site as of this time. If applicable, MTFs and their corresponding departments should be guarding SCPs inside the facility— preventing them from escaping.

Civilian Placement: All civilians should remain inside of the location during the roleplay.

MTF/Foundation Grouping: Group all MTFs and foundation personnel together outside the facility to maintain a visible security presence and coordinate response efforts.

Breach and Clear: Once the convoy is prepared and all teams are in position, the Foundation and MTF will breach into the location and eliminate all threats to secure the area and complete the scenario objectives.

Command — Request Black/SCP Livery

PM Command
:pm [user] Please equip a black/SCP-themed livery or be moderated.

Announcement — Form Convoy

Announcement
:h Create a neat SCP convoy outside of the Police Station!

Peacetimer — Prevent Random Shootout

Peacetimer
:PT 120-750

Announcement — Clear Civilians

Announcement
:h Civilians must leave the current area now or be moderated!

Site Lockdown Announcements

Site Lockdown Warning
:h - - - Warning: SITE-61 is under FULL SITE LOCKDOWN. - - -
Department Report
:h All medical, fire, science, report to designated evacuation/lockdown zones.
Security Report
:h All security, report to SECTOR A, B. Containment breach.
MTF Entry
:h MTF- designation [. . .] has entered the site. All non-combatant units are to report to their evacuation zone immediately.
Lockdown Lifted
:h The site lockdown has been lifted. Return to normal operations.

SCP Roleplay — Player Roles

Player Role Subdivision

Players in SCP RP divide into the following roles:

  • The Foundation — Includes Security Department, Scientific Department, Medical/Fire Suppression, Mobile Task Forces
  • Whitelisted Teams — Includes MTF Alpha-9, Chaos Insurgency, Global Occult Coalition
  • The Civilians — May attack the foundation site, or may roleplay peacefully

The Foundation

Security Department — Responsible for protecting the majority of the foundation and are the "first responders" in responding to any disturbances internally and externally of the site.

Scientific Department — Responsible for performing "safe-controlled" scientific tests on "civilians"/AKA D-class. They consist of researchers, scientists, etc.

Medical Department/Fire Suppression Department — Responsible for the technical fire-medical related safety of the site. They help to assist with standard fires, injured people, and to contain basic medical/fire related issues.

Mobile Task Force | [MTF] — Consists of highly specialized small teams— usually considered as special forces. They help deal with stronger threats that require expertise and greater help. They assist the Security-Department in containment and site-lockdown operations.

  • MTF NU-7 (Mobile Task Force NU-7) — Armed with heavy machinery and equipment, matching that of a small army in real life.
  • MTF BETA-7 (Mobile Task Force BETA-7) — Specialized in containment of biohazardous breaches.
  • MTF NTF-11 (Mobile Task Force Nine-Tailed-Fox[s]) — Specialized in containment of most SCPs.
  • MTF Alpha-01 (Mobile Task Force ALPHA-1) — Specialized team— responsible for securing O5 council during a site breach/other purposes.
  • [WL] MTF ALPHA-09 (Mobile Task Force ALPHA-9) — Designation, "Last Hope." This is the best of the best in the entire SCP Foundation. They utilize SCPs in the containment of others. They are truly the Last Hope.

Whitelisted Teams

[WL] CI (Chaos Insurgency) — The CI used to be a specialized team— given the same duty as ALPHA-1. They however— opposed the Foundation's interests. Hostile to Foundation

[WL] GOC (Global Occult Coalition) — This is a fictional neutral based team. Similar to how NATO and the UN works. They believe that all SCPs should be destroyed for the safety of humanity. Even if that means destroying the Foundation. Hostile to Foundation

The Civilians

Civilians may choose to do one of two things: attack the foundation site, or they may roleplay peacefully.

Border Roleplay

ℹ️
What is Border Roleplay?

Border Roleplay (often abbreviated as Border RP), is a type of Roleplay which involves roleplaying a border within two spaces, with controls on vehicles, peoples and goods passing between the two spaces, hence involving the participation of both LEOs and normal players.

Border Roleplay is not our primary roleplay for our staff members to host, you only host this if there is a lack of staff or a lack of people in game to host a presidential roleplay.

Role Information

There isn't really a need for an host or any other type of role, all staff members on-duty should help out by monitoring the border area, ensuring there is no rule breakers, making frequent announcements about the location of the border and, finally, taking mod calls that show up.

Allowed Locations

  • High Rock Park (Main Entrance)
  • Highways

Useful Announcements

Announcement — When RP Changes to Border
:m The roleplay has been changed to border at [Location].

If DOT is needed, add: "DOT is needed to help setup the border."

Announcement — Every 1–5 Minutes
:h Welcome to SCPERLC, the current roleplay is Border and it is located at [location].
Announcement — Every ~10 Minutes or During Chaos
:h Friendly reminder, random shooting or killing is not allowed at the border.

Roleplay Rules

  • Civilians must stop at the check points and follow the police's instructions
  • All sirens are prohibited
  • Ramming is prohibited
  • RDM, or more generally, random shooting is not allowed at the border area
  • It is allowed for civilians to start a motivated fight with the police at the border

Border Roleplay Start Procedure

ℹ️
Note

If you already know the basics, skip to this page to learn how to start a Border RP.

How to Start a Border RP

  1. Select a location — Choose from the allowed locations (High Rock Park Main Entrance or Highways)
  2. Set up the border — Use DOT workers to set up the border, or have HR+ members load in one of the layouts (HR+ members only, as they have admin commands). If no HR+ is online, ask DOT members to set up the border or request a SHR+ member to use API commands to load the layout.
  3. Set up checkpoints — Create 2 checkpoints (one for each direction of the road), including spaces for:
    • ID check checkpoint for officers
    • Vehicle search area
    • Parking area for staff and service cars (kept clean and out of the way)
  4. Announce the RP start — Use the following command to notify all players (replace [Location] with the actual location):
    Border RP Announcement
    :m The roleplay has been changed to border at [Location].

    Important: Limit yourself to one M announcement per RP change to avoid frequent UI messages. If players don't show up, use H-type announcements first, and only use M announcements again if no one shows up after several attempts.

  5. Handle the RP — As a staff member, your main job is to ensure everyone follows the rules. Typical procedure involves players crossing the border and getting their vehicle and personal data inspected by police staff present at the border.
⚠️
Note

You can use the "copy" button on the command boxes to directly copy and paste the command in-game.

Zombie Roleplay

Zombie roleplay is one of our most popular roleplay scenarios! It involves surviving a zombie apocalypse, managing resources, and working together to stay alive.

🧟
About Zombie RP

Zombie roleplay simulates a post-apocalyptic world overrun by zombies. Players must scavenge for supplies, build shelters, and survive against the undead horde.

Key Elements

  • Survival — Eliminate zombies (civilians)
  • Defenses — The police team can set up defenses to reduce the chances of being infected
  • Coordination — Teamwork can prevent the zombies from overrunning the police

Staff Roles in Zombie RP

  • Event Coordinators — Set up and manage zombie events
  • Zone Moderators — Ensure fair gameplay in different zones

Staff Responsibility: Staff members are expected to monitor the injuring of officers and the weapons that zombies use to ensure that only knives and punches are being used against police staff, maintaining fairness and integrity in the roleplay.

Rules for Zombie RP

Infected Police

If players on the Police Team are injured/damaged by a civilian, they must switch to the civilian side and attack the Police Team as zombies. This is a mandatory rule to maintain the integrity of the zombie roleplay scenario.

Civilian Attack Restrictions

Civilians must only use knives or punches to injure officers during zombie roleplay. No firearms, explosives, or other weapons are permitted when targeting police staff.

Zombie Appearance

Recommended: All civilians playing as zombies should use a green skintone to clearly identify themselves as undead participants in the roleplay.

Police Team Status

Important: The police team will be considered non-infected during zombie roleplay. They are not part of the undead horde and should be clearly distinguishable from zombies.

Tips for Staff

  • Clear the way — Staff must keep pathways clear between the two teams, or they might get caught in the crossfire
  • Handle modcalls — Be prepared to respond to moderation calls during the roleplay
Player Briefing

Staff should also inform players of the key elements (Survival, Defenses, and Coordination) before the roleplay begins to ensure everyone understands the scenario and objectives.

Note

This is meant to be a semi-chaotic RP. You are expected to monitor and let the events play out.

Normal Roleplay

⛔
Important Warning

Normal Roleplay should almost never be used. It is considered a last resort and should only be hosted when all available staff members are absent and there is no other option. Our server's primary focus is on structured roleplay events (President RP, SCP RP, Border RP, Zombie RP). Do not host Normal Roleplay if any staff members are available to host a specialized RP instead.

General Roleplay Guidelines

Normal roleplay is the day-to-day roleplay that occurs in our community. Here are the key guidelines:

Basic Rules

  • Stay in character at all times
  • Do not break character unnecessarily
  • Respect other players' roleplay
  • Do not force roleplay on unwilling players

Accepted Roleplay Types

  • Business and commerce
  • Law enforcement and emergency services
  • Government and politics
  • Medical and healthcare
  • Civilian life scenarios

Prohibited Roleplay

  • NSFW (Not Safe For Work) content
  • Banned roleplay (bomb, torture, etc.)
  • Extremism or racism
  • Exploitation or abuse roleplay
⚠️
Remember

All roleplay must comply with Roblox Platform Terms of Service and server rules.

Arbiter Pass

What is an Arbiter Pass?

An Arbiter Pass is a special permission that allows staff members to perform certain actions that require higher authority. It is typically granted to senior staff members.

Requirements

  • Minimum rank of Moderator
  • Clean disciplinary record
  • Recommendation from a superior
  • Completion of additional training
⛔
Important

You are not supposed to ask HR for an Arbiter Pass. It will be given to the approiate staff members when the situation requires it.

Privileges

  • Access to advanced moderation commands
  • Authority to make final decisions on disputes
  • Ability to override certain punishments
  • Access to confidential information
⚠️
Important

Abuse of the Arbiter Pass will result in immediate revocation and potential rank removal.

🏠 Home / Additional Information / Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions

How do I get promoted?

Promotions are based on performance, shift hours, and recommendations from superiors. Consistent good behavior and dedication are key.

What commands can I use?

The commands available to you depend on your rank. See the Command Usage page for details on command letter requirements.

How do I handle a specific situation?

Refer to the Punishment Guide and Roleplay Guidelines for specific procedures. When in doubt, consult your superiors.

What happens if I make a mistake?

Mistakes happen. Report them to your superiors, learn from them, and move forward. The staff team is here to support you.

What is SSD/SSU?

SSD stands for Server Start Up and SSU stands for Server Shutdown. These are commands used to restart or shut down the server. Only staff members with appropriate permissions (HR+ or higher) should use these commands, and they should be used responsibly during low-activity periods to minimize disruption to players.

Melonly Usage

ℹ️
Melonly Information

Our server uses Melonly as moderation service, therefore Melonly is a must: you are required to be in it and use it when going on duty. Here we will show how to join it, what it is, how to use it and such.

Leaking our Melonly invite will result in moderation action against anyone who is involved.

Tips You Should Consider

  • Only HR+ can execute ban bolos. In the events you see a ban-worthy action, instead of waiting for a HR to ban them, kick the player and log a "Ban Bolo" on them using Melonly.
  • When logging a punishment like LTAP, please make sure to also include what they did before they LTAPed, in the punishment's reason.
  • When logging a warning, kick or a ban bolo, please take a second to see if you've typed the correct name in.
  • Please note, you must use decent SPaG in punishment logs as community members can see them! Not doing so will result in staff infractions.
  • We do not require you to use high grammar in punishments, but make sure that sentences have a meaning, and that you are not using slang terms.

Examples

Good Examples
  • Usage of banned weapon
  • He gonna insult me

You can avoid putting punctuation at the end of the sentence and other minor things like this, though you must make sure that your sentence looks well-written.

Get Roblox ID

How to Find a Player's Roblox ID

There are plenty of ways to get a player's Roblox ID. Here's the easiest method:

Using Their Profile Link

If you have the player's Roblox profile link, you can extract their ID directly from the URL. A Roblox link looks something like this:

🔗Example
https://www.roblox.com/users/2269104615/profile

The link is formed by various parts divided by slashes. The important part is [user's ID] — that number is the player's Roblox ID.

Note: You may see different formats (with or without country tags like `/it/`, with or without `/profile` at the end), but the ID number is always the same sequence of digits before `/profile` or at the end of the URL.

How to Get a Player's Profile Link

  • Search their name in Roblox's search bar under the "People" section — the link will appear in your browser's address bar
  • Click on their profile name to open their profile page — the link will always be in your browser's address bar

TIP: If you're unsure, always paste the link in your browser and check if the displayed username is correct before using the ID.

Why You Need It

Knowing a player's Roblox ID is essential for moderation actions, punishment records, and communication.
